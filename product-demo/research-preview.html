Example report · fictional industrial B2B supplier

The following sections show the intended analytical deliverable and how verified facts, hypotheses and missing evidence would be separated.

01

Company profile

Offerings, business model, locations and publicly observable capabilities.

Illustrative hypothesis: the supplier serves equipment manufacturers and industrial operators.
02

Markets and segments

Market definitions, buying situations, demand drivers and geographic scope.

Compare OEM demand with replacement and service demand.
03

Competitors and alternatives

Competitor identification, comparable offerings, routes to market and substitution.

Compare direct suppliers with in-house solutions.
04

Products and positioning

Observable product claims, differentiation, pricing where public, and supporting evidence.

Test whether delivery reliability is a meaningful differentiator.
05

Opportunities and risks

Evidence-linked hypotheses and competing explanations, with explicit uncertainty.

Potential opportunity: specialist niches. Risk: limited buying-group access.
06

Evidence and next checks

Source register: URL, access date, claim, confidence and contradictions. No sources collected for this fictional sample.

Unknown: margins, conversion, customer concentration, retention and actual market share.

Next professional step

Validate the unknowns with appropriate external and internal data before choosing a strategy. The report does not establish a company's internal marketing maturity.
